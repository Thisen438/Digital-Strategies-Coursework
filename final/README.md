## Biggest corrections from the citation check
1. **PDPA**: it was *not* "fully in force since March 2025". Its core duties start on **1 January 2027** (Gazette 2498/16, 22 Jul 2026). That date falls inside your roadmap.
2. **Net Plus tariff**: LKR 15.81–23.11/kWh by system size, not "about LKR 17".
3. **EPR**: the National Environmental (Amendment) Act No. 15 of 2026 now exists, so "no mandatory EPR" is out of date.
4. **Electricity**: the 18% rise from 11 May 2026 also covers GP-2/GP-3 general-purpose users. That is probably IIT's category, so check its bill.
5. **Inflation calculation**: 26% is the rise in prices. The budget's buying power falls by about 21% (about 14% at 5% inflation).
6. **Not verified (fix or remove)**: about 8,200 students; oil "near USD 89"; the 70% youth figure; Daily Mirror (2026a); Rome2rio.
